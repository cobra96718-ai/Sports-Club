About PTU Sports Club

The I. K. Gujral Punjab Technical University (IKGPTU) Sports Department promotes sports among students by organizing inter-college tournaments, coaching camps, and leadership training, and it provides various sports facilities like grounds and indoor courts. The department also awards financial incentives to medal winners and provides sports kits, aiming to foster overall student development and a strong sense of sportsmanship.Student‑run, coach‑guided, and open to all skill levels. Come learn, compete and grow.
